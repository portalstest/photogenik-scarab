2. Ultra-Thin Stag-Beetle Mandibles + Dual-Use Suction Gripper

How an 8.1 cm Scarab picks up a 2.7 g (40 mm) ping-pong ball, rings, or flat Instax photos without blinding its belly sensors or adding a second servo.

3D Curved / Spherical Grip +0.95 g

🪲 25°–30° Carbon-Wire Stag Mandibles

Hanging a 40 mm ball under the center belly blocks the PMW3901 Optical Flow (42°) and VL53L3CX ToF laser (27°), causing an instant crash, while hovering overhead blows a 2.7 g ball away. Instead, two hair-thin 0.5 mm carbon-fiber rods project 30 mm forward at a 25°–30° chin angle. The Scarab lands 6 cm behind the ball and scoots forward, snapping the ball passively past its equator (0 W holding power, 100% clear sensor & prop cones).

🧲 Dual-Use Wall + Pickup Suction Cup

The 0.8 g TPU-gimbaled micro-suction cup on the front chin sits on a 90° passive flip-detent hinge: angled forward, it locks onto vertical walls/mirrors; flipped down 90°, the Scarab lands gently on flat smooth items (1.2 g Instax Mini photo, 1.8 g playing card, 0.8 g guitar pick, ID badge, or sticky note) to seal a passive vacuum grip and carry them across the room.

Zero-Motor Mid-Air Release 0.02 g Dyneema Thread

🪽 85° Wing-Flare "Lost-Motion" Drop

One 0.1 mm Dyneema tendon links the existing 1.7 g wing servo to both grippers via a slotted cam. During normal flight (0°–65° wing angle), the thread stays slack. Commanding an 85° Full Wing Flare over a target cup or hand pulls the tendon 3 mm—simultaneously spreading the carbon mandibles to drop a ping-pong ball AND lifting a 1 mm edge-peel tab on the suction cup lip to break vacuum and drop a card!

4. Weekend-by-Weekend Build Checklist

Weekend 1 · ~$35 Stage-0 Desk Build
Build the Animatronic Scarab + Spring Nest First
  1. Print at Boelter Makerspace: Print the two 75×38 mm curved Elytra domes in 0.4 mm Spiral Vase Mode (0.9 g each) plus the Spring Launch Nest bowl.
  2. Assemble Y-Linkage: Mount the 1.7 g linear servo + 0.5 mm music-wire Y-pushrod so a 6 mm slide sweeps both Elytra open 45° and pulls the Mylar sub-wings out to 18.0 cm.
  3. Wire the Staring Eye: Put the XIAO ESP32S3 camera under the front pronotum notch so it peeks out and streams Wi-Fi video when the shell cracks open 15°.
  4. Scale Check: Weigh the entire clip-on shell assembly on a $12 (0.01 g) pocket scale — verify it is ≤ 4.9 g before flight.
Weekend 2 · +$50 StampFly Integration
Spring Jump-Start, Hover & Wall-Sit
  1. Snap Onto StampFly: Clip the 4.9 g Scarab shell bridge onto the center X-frame of the 8.1 cm StampFly — keeping all 4 circular air intakes 100% unobstructed.
  2. Calibrate the 0.18 J Nest Spring: Set the desk cradle spring compression so releasing the latch tosses the 40.7 g Scarab ~40 cm straight up along two short guide rails.
  3. Apex Hover Capture: Trigger the StampFly's IMU + ToF laser to spin up the shrouded rotors as it leaves the cradle and lock a 40 cm hover at zero vertical velocity (tuck shells during transit; unfold to 18 cm in settled hover).
  4. Press-to-Perch Wall Test: Press the front TPU-gimbaled micro-suction pad onto a glass/mirror panel (or magnetic wall target), cut motors to 0 W, and crack the shell 15° to watch the room in silence!
