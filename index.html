3. Weekend-by-Weekend Build Checklist

Weekend 1 · ~$35 Stage-0 Desk Build
Build the Animatronic Scarab + Spring Nest First
  1. Print at Boelter Makerspace: Print the two 75×38 mm curved Elytra domes in 0.4 mm Spiral Vase Mode (0.9 g each) plus the Spring Launch Nest bowl.
  2. Assemble Y-Linkage: Mount the 1.7 g linear servo + 0.5 mm music-wire Y-pushrod so a 6 mm slide sweeps both Elytra open 45° and pulls the Mylar sub-wings out to 18.0 cm.
  3. Wire the Staring Eye: Put the XIAO ESP32S3 camera under the front pronotum notch so it peeks out and streams Wi-Fi video when the shell cracks open 15°.
  4. Scale Check: Weigh the entire clip-on shell assembly on a $12 (0.01 g) pocket scale — verify it is ≤ 4.9 g before flight.
Weekend 2 · +$50 StampFly Integration
Spring Jump-Start, Hover & Wall-Sit
  1. Snap Onto StampFly: Clip the 4.9 g Scarab shell bridge onto the center X-frame of the 8.1 cm StampFly — keeping all 4 circular air intakes 100% unobstructed.
  2. Calibrate the 0.18 J Nest Spring: Set the desk cradle spring compression so releasing the latch tosses the 40.7 g Scarab ~40 cm straight up along two short guide rails.
  3. Apex Hover Capture: Trigger the StampFly's IMU + ToF laser to spin up the shrouded rotors as it leaves the cradle and lock a 40 cm hover at zero vertical velocity (tuck shells during transit; unfold to 18 cm in settled hover).
  4. Press-to-Perch Wall Test: Press the front TPU-gimbaled micro-suction pad onto a glass/mirror panel (or magnetic wall target), cut motors to 0 W, and crack the shell 15° to watch the room in silence!
