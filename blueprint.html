3. 85° Wing-Flare Tendon Release

A 0.08 mm Dyneema Y-tendon connects the existing 1.7 g wing servo to both the suction peel-tab (branch L1) and mandible caliper arms (branch L2). During normal flight (0°–65°), both branches stay slack. Over the target cup, commanding an 85° wing flare pulls L2 by 3.0 mm—spreading the mandibles to 45 mm and dropping the ball dead-center!
